About Wren

I'm a reader first and most other things second. Most of what's on this site comes out of the same handful of obsessions: old books, invented worlds, and the small, deliberate objects people make around both — maps, marginalia, hand-bound zines.

By day I write and tinker; by evening I'm usually somewhere in the middle of three books at once, at least one of them a reread. This site is where I keep the notes I don't want to lose.
